about

Born in post-Soviet, democratic Bulgaria, I graduated from a semi-art high school in Sofia, then spent my bachelor years in the northernmost corner of Italy before landing in the Netherlands for my master's degree.

Somewhere along the way, I started working with data through infographics, laying out research and turning plain information into visual stories. What started as an exploration out of curiousity eventually became my job which I absolutely love!
