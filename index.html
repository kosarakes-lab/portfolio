Born in post-Soviet, democratic Bulgaria, I graduated from a semi-art high school in Sofia, then spent my bachelor years in the northernmost corner of Italy before landing in the Netherlands for my master's degree. Somewhere along the way, I started working with data through infographics, laying out research and turning plain information into visual stories. I focus mainly on issues of social justice, politics, and media, using data visualization, motion, and editorial design to make critical topics legible and engaging.
