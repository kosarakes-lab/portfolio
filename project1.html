University Project
{ 2025 }
Design Academy Eindhoven

The Social Museum explores how people interact with artworks in photos shared on Instagram, uncovering the performative side of museum visits. Are visitors truly engaging with the art, or are they performing in front of the camera to have a curated presence for social media? What defines engagement in this context? By examining thousands of Instagram images from the six largest contemporary art museums of 2024—both those posted by the institutions and those shared by visitors—we identified patterns in how individuals behave in a gallery setting. Through a curated selection of images featuring solitary visitors in the museum spaces, we categorized and analyzed the ways people position themselves in relation to artworks. This research-led installation transforms our findings into an interactive space where you can explore these patterns firsthand. As you move, reflect on your choices—are you drawn to engagement, performance, or something in between? The Social Museum invites you to question how social media shapes our collective experience of contemporary art.
